## 2026-08-27 refinement — child topology cannot outlive its survival certificate

Three user production failures showed that the proactive line-survival architecture still had a topology-lifetime hole even after emerge→spread was corrected. Under-floor recovery fragmentation could export unresolved survival debt to singleton siblings, a young branch could replace its parent before every child had atomically secured the full structural maneuver through four modules, and a later traceback could erase a correctly committed fan child's own survival maneuver because its rollback floor remained at zero.

Current production therefore makes the survival certificate part of topology ownership:

- recovery fragmentation is forbidden while the family still carries unresolved <4-module survival debt;
- young MAIN fan/branch topology below four modules is committed only together with a rendered-lane-legal maneuver that takes every child lane through the unchanged four-module survival floor;
- the committed structural maneuver becomes that child's `recovery_floor_segments`; ordinary recovery may reroute later geometry but cannot rewind through the certificate while leaving the child topology alive.

The ordinary per-line floor remains **4 modules**, not four straight modules, and the separate >=8-module side-progress requirement remains one outcome per launch side. Exact user-failing sample seeds `1638463186752876422`, `13995978127231202083`, and `3524258640260304746` all pass on the final source under the explicitly inferred `1:6 @ 0.35` MAIN-only reproduction, with every launch visible and zero hard MAIN debt. ## 2026-08-27 refinement — emerge first, then spread; no synthetic whole-bus bend

A user-visible review plus a new valid-seed stalled-side failure showed that the earlier survival-floor correction had left the launch topology internally inconsistent. The four-module *straight* requirement was gone, but the fan was still gated behind four modules of unsplit root egress. That allowed a complete 9–24 line bus to turn as one object before it could partition. Ordinary MAIN scoring also retained a synthetic turn reward after two straight gestures, so the resulting bend cadence was genuinely systematic rather than merely a seeded outcome.

Current production separates the concepts correctly:

- gesture zero is the perpendicular emergence trunk;
- immediately after that physical emergence, the root may transactionally fan into several balanced contiguous cohorts;
- the child transaction itself proves every lane reaches the unchanged four-module hard survival floor;
- legal fan topology is selected from seeded/open-space-scored viable alternatives, not one fixed preferred layout with fallback-only permutation;
- fan legality is checked on the actual rendered child lanes, avoiding false conflicts from overlapping coarse cohort envelopes near a legal peel;
- ordinary MAIN has mild directional inertia but no fixed "time to bend" reward;
- the last live outcome of a launch side carries live >=8-module side-progress debt;
- different launch sides of one chip are legitimate connection families after egress, while same-side siblings remain one family.

The maintained stress suite then found a final sibling loophole on sample seed `1909786089031391816`: an exemption intended for the first *straight parallel* fan rebase could survive while a child was blocked and accidentally legalize its later first turn across a sibling corridor. The exemption now requires the candidate direction itself to remain straight. The active regression uses real crossing geometry and rejects that diagonal.

Qualification: **175/175 + stress 1/1**, plus three fresh square production renders with zero stalled sides, zero MAIN short terminations, zero unaccounted launches, and many viable fan alternatives. Current renderer SHA-256: `4c87378867645aa90727b77ea94e98eb59c6a6f4f993a45cbbf786b5239afc8e`.

## Causal sequence

1. Broad late rescue and acceptance-style approaches were rejected because anti-line-murder must be proactive construction intelligence.
2. Exact traces localized a recurring corpse mechanism to viable newborn structural children repeatedly losing their mandatory first rebase in same-round arbitration.
3. One-HOLD anti-starvation fairness substantially reduced short visible terminations, but its first combined version caused two late preflight repairs on 0.5/seed-102.

5. Replacing that late helper with bounded shared-snapshot atomic settlement removed the illegal sibling commits and preflight repairs while retaining the proactive first-rebase fairness.
6. A behavior-metric audit found an apples-to-oranges comparison in earlier notes. Final qualification uses rendered visible route length from `pathway_main_outcome_by_trace` for every source/seed.
7. Conflict-combination counters looked superlinear, but direct profiling showed the HOLD solver itself was negligible CPU. The candidate was therefore judged on deterministic category work plus clean overall CPU/work, not on one scary counter.
8. Overall normalized MAIN CPU/work improved from 1.333x to 1.204x over 0.75 -> 0.5 on seeds 102+104, and promoted 0.5 -> 0.35 finished at 1.030x.
9. The expanded 148-test release gate and maintained stress gate passed before and after promotion. Historical known MAIN rejection seeds 1.0/103, 0.75/103, and 0.75/105 then emitted complete boards without seed skipping.
